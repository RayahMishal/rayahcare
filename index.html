Welcome to RayahCare

RayahCare is a Clinical Decision Support System (CDSS) prototype designed to help healthcare providers review medical data efficiently and consistently. Whether you are working with paediatric or adult patients, RayahCare reads the clinical notes you enter and highlights what needs attention first.

It combines a validated early-warning score, red-flag detection in free-text notes and laboratory flags into one explainable summary, so every suggestion shows the rule behind it.

Key Features:

  • Supports patient ages from 0 months up to 120 years (the NEWS2 score applies to adults 16+; paediatric scoring is not included)
  • Reads unstructured medical notes and extracts vital signs, labs and red flags
  • Accepts a medical image, previewed locally (no image analysis in this prototype)
  • Gives an attention level with every reason listed, for the clinician to confirm
  • User-friendly interface with pastel theme and smooth navigation

Educational prototype. Fictional data only. Not a medical device; it never replaces clinical judgement.
